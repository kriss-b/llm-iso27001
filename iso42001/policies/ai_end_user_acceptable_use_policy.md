# AI End-User Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the rules external users shall follow when using the Company's AI systems, and the grounds on which access may be restricted. It protects users, third parties, and the systems themselves from misuse, in alignment with **ISO 42001:2023 (Control A.9 — Responsible use of AI systems)**.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to:
- All external users of the Company's AI systems, including customers, their own end users, and anyone accessing the systems through an API or integration.
- All AI systems the Company makes available externally, as recorded in the [AI Systems Register](../../run/registers/ai_systems_register.md).

Staff use of AI tools for Company work is governed by the [Acceptable Use Policy](../../policies/acceptable_use_policy.md), not this policy.

---

## Policy Statements

### Permitted Use
- AI systems shall be used for their documented intended purpose, within the limits published for the system.
- Any other use requires prior written approval from the Company.

### Prohibited Use
Users shall not:
- Bypass or attempt to bypass safeguards, including jailbreaks, prompt injection, and obfuscated or encoded instructions.
- Extract or attempt to extract system prompts, training data, model weights, credentials, or other users' data.
- Generate unlawful, harmful, deceptive, or rights-infringing content.
- Scrape, overload, or probe the AI endpoints, whether manually or by automation.
- Reverse engineer the system, or use its outputs to train a competing model.
- Present outputs as human-authored, as professional advice, or as the sole basis of a decision affecting a person's rights, safety, employment, or access to services.

### Human Oversight
- Outputs may be inaccurate or incomplete and are not verified fact.
- The user remains accountable for any decision taken or content published on the basis of an output.

### Reporting and Enforcement
- Harmful outputs, safety concerns, and suspected vulnerabilities shall be reported without delay, via the contact route given in the system's user information.
- The Company may rate-limit, suspend, or terminate access where this policy is breached or where continued use presents a risk to users, third parties, or the system.

---

## Review and Updates
This policy will be reviewed **annually** or after significant changes to the Company's AI systems, their intended use, or applicable regulations.
